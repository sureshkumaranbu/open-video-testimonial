# Customers

**Customers** (the `customers` section, on by default) keeps one record for each person in a project, so everything they sent and were sent is in one place. Only you and your team see it: no public page, widget or feed ever shows a customer record.

## Where records come from

A record is made, or found by email address, every time a testimonial arrives: through a form, added by hand, imported from a CSV or spreadsheet, or imported from a review site. Someone without an email address (most review sites don't share one) gets a record of their own. Testimonials from before customer records existed, and ones added through the API, are linked by an hourly job.

A later testimonial never overwrites what a record already says; it only fills in what's missing.

## The list

**Customers** lists everyone in the project, newest first, with how many testimonials, private feedback and invitations each has. Search by name, email or company.

## A customer's page

- **Testimonials**: every one of theirs, with its status, stars and source, and a link that opens it in the inbox.
- **Private feedback**: what they sent privately (a private question, or the low-rating page), linked to them or sent with their address.
- **Invitations and campaign emails**: personal requests and campaign emails to their address, with when they were sent, clicked and answered.
- **Rewards**: the reward codes they claimed.
- **Details**: their email, job title, company and website. **Edit** changes the record only: their testimonials keep what they wrote (that's what they consented to).

Three ways to ask them for more:

- **Invite to a form**: a personal link to one of your forms, emailed now (when email is set up) or copied to share yourself. It appears in the Requests tab, with reminders if you've set them. Someone who already has an open invitation, or unsubscribed, can't be invited again.
- **Ask for a video**: the same, offering first the forms whose main question asks for a video. If none does, set a question's answer to **Video** in the form builder.
- **Add testimonial**: the usual **Add testimonial** dialog with their details filled in, linked to them.

## Possible duplicates

Two testimonials of the same person can be merged into one in the inbox; see [the inbox guide](inbox.md#possible-duplicates-and-merging).
