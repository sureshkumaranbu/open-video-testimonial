**Why placeholders and not stock footage.** The plan was short clips of people talking to camera under the Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit or CC0 licences. The cloud environment that built this has a network policy that blocks every stock-video host we tried:
- pexels.com
- pixabay.com
- mixkit.co
- commons.wikimedia.org
- archive.org
- videvo.net
- coverr.co

Each returned a proxy 403. Rather than present anything misleading, the clips are honest synthetic placeholders that contain no real person.

**Fonts.** The text in the clips is set in Inter (SIL Open Font License 1.1), from the build machine's system fonts.

**Swapping in real footage later.** Put licensed clips at the same paths (`public/samples/<id>.webm` / `.mp4`, poster `<id>.jpg`, 960×600 or the same 16:10 ratio) and add a row here with the source URL, author and licence for each. Even with real footage, keep the "Sample" labels: the quotes on the home page are fictional and must not read as an endorsement by the person on screen.

## Sample testimonials (text)

The names, roles, companies and quotes used on the home page are fictional. Any resemblance to real people or businesses is unintended. They are always shown with a "Sample" label and an "illustration" caption.
